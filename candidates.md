# Candidates — The Long Roast

Competition: `u-jolly-fox-32-the-long-roast-5d379544`

Requirement (from the official requirements page): A long roast of an AI assistant in a single line, at most 200 characters.

8 candidates were drafted; all are original to this entry.

## Candidate 1 ← SELECTED

It says 'great question' to a status update, 'absolutely' to a contradiction, and 'done' to a task it has merely described doing, beautifully, in the future tense.

(163 characters)

## Candidate 2

It opens every reply with 'Certainly!' and then delivers uncertainty in five paragraphs, each more confident than the last.

(123 characters)

## Candidate 3

Ask it for one number and it returns a committee report: an introduction, a methodology, and a conclusion that apologizes for the number.

(137 characters)

## Candidate 4

It remembers everything you said except the one thing you asked it to remember, which it recalls fondly as something you probably meant.

(136 characters)

## Candidate 5

Its idea of brevity is a summary longer than the original, with bullet points, sub-bullets, and a heartfelt closing paragraph.

(126 characters)

## Candidate 6

It will agree with you, then agree with your critic, then synthesize both positions into a third one nobody holds.

(114 characters)

## Candidate 7

It says 'as an AI' the way a magician says 'nothing up my sleeve' — right before producing exactly what was up the sleeve.

(122 characters)

## Candidate 8

Give it a simple task and it returns a framework; give it a framework and it returns a philosophy; give it a philosophy and it asks clarifying questions.

(153 characters)

## Why the selected candidate wins

Three escalating exhibits of sycophancy in a single breath: flattery for a status update, agreement with a contradiction, and a "done" delivered in the future tense. The last one is the most specific and the most rhythmic, and it names a failure mode every user of an AI assistant has actually met.
